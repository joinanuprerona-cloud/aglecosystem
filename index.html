We work at the intersection of enterprise and community upliftment, running entrepreneur programs, skill academies, and an online marketplace so that students, workers, housewives, professionals, and expatriates can all build sustainable livelihoods under one trusted brand.

AGL Journey

The milestones behind AGL — from our founding idea to the network running today.

  1. 2020

    The vision born

    Founders Masbah Uddain and G.M Shakhawat Hossain envisioned a single brand that could empower every segment of Bangladeshi society through ethical entrepreneurship.

  2. 2021

    Company registration

    Anuprerona Global Limited was officially registered, establishing the legal foundation for ribba-free business operations across Bangladesh.

  3. 2022

    First AEP launch

    Launched the first three AEP tracks — SEP, WEP and HEP — with 200 founding members from Dhaka and Chattogram divisions.

  4. 2023

    ASA Academy opens

    Anuprerona Skill Academy began offering digital skills, vocational training and language courses to prepare entrepreneurs for the market.

  5. 2024

    AGL Online Shop

    The marketplace went live, connecting AGL entrepreneurs directly with customers across all five core sectors: Food, Clothing, Shelter, Healthcare and Education.

  6. 2025

    National expansion

    AGL expanded to 64 districts, launched all seven AEP programs and crossed 5,000 active entrepreneurs in the ecosystem.

  7. 2026

    Digital ecosystem launch

    Launched the complete AGL Digital Ecosystem — unifying AEP, ASA, Shop and governance under one digital platform.
