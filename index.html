Our Evolution

From Startup Vision to a Unified Corporate Identity

Anuprerona Global Limited (AGL) represents a journey of entrepreneurial vision, strategic transformation, organizational development, and long-term ambition. Its evolution reflects a continuous effort to establish a structured business ecosystem that connects entrepreneurs, professionals, skilled workers, business partners, and communities through ethical business practices.

  1. October 15, 2025

    The Beginning: Bangla Startup Network (BSN)

    The journey began with the establishment of Bangla Startup Network (BSN), an entrepreneurial initiative intended to create opportunities for startup development, networking, collaboration, and business growth.

    Strategic Significance: Establishing the initial entrepreneurial concept and laying the groundwork for a broader business network.

  2. November 06, 2025

    Strategic Rebranding: Ashtha Partners Limited (APL)

    On November 6, 2025, the initiative transitioned to the identity of Ashtha Partners Limited (APL), reflecting a new stage in its organizational direction toward partnership, trust, and collaborative business development.

    Strategic Significance: Advancing the organizational identity toward partnership, trust, and collaborative business development.

  3. November 23, 2025

    Organizational Transition: Agrochinta Private Limited

    On November 23, 2025, the journey entered another phase under the name Agrochinta Private Limited, continuing to shape broader business ambitions and organizational structure.

    Strategic Significance: Continuing organizational development and moving toward a more consolidated long-term business vision.

  4. December 02, 2025

    The Defining Transition: Anuprerona Global Limited (AGL)

    On December 2, 2025, the organization adopted the identity of Anuprerona Global Limited (AGL), establishing the corporate identity associated with its broader vision for an interconnected business ecosystem.

    Build Future Together One Brand. One Name. One Network. Empowering Youth Through Work. Ethical Islamic Business Principles Food Clothes Shelter Healthcare Education

    Strategic Significance: Establishing a unified corporate identity and articulating a broader vision for an interconnected business ecosystem.

  5. December 07, 2025

    Official Incorporation and Legal Foundation

    December 7, 2025, marks the official incorporation date of Anuprerona Global Limited (AGL), according to the organization's stated registration information — formalized under the Registrar of Joint Stock Companies and Firms (RJSC) in Bangladesh.

    Strategic Significance: Formalizing the company's legal identity and establishing a foundation for accountable corporate development.

  6. The Next Chapter

    Building the Future Together

    AGL's future direction is centered on five essential sectors — Food, Clothes, Shelter, Healthcare, and Education — connecting learning with practical skills, skills with entrepreneurship, entrepreneurship with business, and business with income generation and employment.

    Our Commitment: Build Future Together — One Brand. One Name. One Network.
